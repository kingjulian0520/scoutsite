FAQ

Common questions

How is this different from just sending coaches my highlight video?+

A video attachment is one file with no context. A ScoutSite gives a coach your stats, position, grad year, schedule and film all in one place — and it's a link they can open instantly on their phone, not a download they have to wait on.

Can you update the site as I get new film or stats?+

Yes — that's what the optional monthly hosting/updates add-on covers. Send new footage or stats whenever you have them and we'll update the live site; your link never changes, so anything you've already shared stays current.

Do I need to know how to code or use a website builder?+

No. You send us your info and video through a simple form, we design and build the entire site, and you approve a preview before anything goes live.

How fast can I get a site live?+

Basic sites are typically delivered within 5 business days, Pro within 3. If you've got a camp or a coach's visit coming up, mention the date and we'll do our best to get you live before it.

Do coaches actually prefer this over an email with video attached?+

Coaches and scouts go through dozens of recruiting emails a day. A clean, fast-loading link that shows exactly what they're looking for — position, measurables, film — gets opened and remembered a lot more often than an email with three video files attached.
